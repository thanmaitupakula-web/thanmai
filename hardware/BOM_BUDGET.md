## Cost-Saving Tips

1. **Buy from Indian suppliers** (Robu.in, Robokidz, Amazon.in)
   - Lower shipping costs
   - Local support
   - No import duties

2. **Use open-source software**
   - Arduino IDE (free)
   - ROS/Python (free)
   - OpenCV (free)

3. **DIY gripper**
   - Instead of ₹2000+ commercial gripper
   - Build with servo + 3D printed parts (~₹800)

4. **Shared components with team**
   - Raspberry Pi can be purchased together
   - Share power supplies
   - Reuse cables across projects

5. **Second-hand market**
   - OLX, Facebook Marketplace
   - Old robot arms/servos (50-70% off)
   - But budget new for reliability

6. **Sponsor support**
   - Approach local robotics companies
   - Request academic discounts
   - Some may donate components

7. **Avoid premium brands**
   - Choose budget alternatives (not low-quality)
   - Still get decent specs
